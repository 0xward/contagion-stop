# Asset logos (local files, no hotlink)

`eth.svg` and `usdc.svg` are the ETH and USDC icons from
[spothq/cryptocurrency-icons](https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons)
(`svg/color/eth.svg`, `svg/color/usdc.svg`, version 0.18.1), used for asset
identification only.

License: CC0 1.0 Universal — the set's `package.json` declares
`"license": "CC0-1.0"` and its `LICENSE.md` is the CC0 1.0 legal code. No
attribution is required; the set's README asks only that the icons are not
claimed as one's own work and that the resource is shared with others.

The images were verified visually before use (15 Nov 2026): ETH is the
white diamond on a blue circle, USDC is the white "$" on a blue circle.

Note: CC0 covers the artwork, not the brands the artwork depicts — the
Ethereum and USDC marks remain their owners' trademarks. The icons are
used here only to identify which asset a vault holds, the standard DeFi
practice.
